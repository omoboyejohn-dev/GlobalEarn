Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining GlobalEarn.

🎁 Do I receive a welcome bonus?

Yes. Every new member receives a $50.00 Welcome Bonus after creating an account.

👥 How does the referral program work?

Invite your friends using your referral link. You earn $3.50 for each successful referral, up to 10 rewarded referrals.

💰 What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum withdrawal amount is $200.00. Once your wallet reaches this amount, you can submit a withdrawal request.

🪙 Which cryptocurrencies are supported?

GlobalEarn supports withdrawals through Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT and Litecoin (LTC).

⏳ How long does a withdrawal take?

After submitting your withdrawal request, it will be reviewed and processed. You can monitor its status in your Transaction History.

Yes. Users from supported countries around the world can create an account and participate in available tasks.
